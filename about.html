Nakaseke International Hospital was founded to improve health care and provide affordable medical services to everyone.

Some of our staff Nakaseke Hospital was founded in the 1960s by Prime Minister Miton Obote`s administration.


It was founded to serve as a rural health facility for Nakaseke District and sorrounding areas


It was strategically important during the National Resistance Movement(NRM)

It became a vital medical center for the National Resistence Armay(NRA)


INITIAL FOUNDING

It was built in the 1960s by the government to provide healthcare to the rural population


Especally in areas like Nakaseke,Luwero,and other nearby districts.


STRATEGIC WARTIME IMPORTANCE


During the NRA bush war in the early 1980s, the hospital served as a major health facility for the fighters.


Its medical director joined the guerrillas and became the cheif medical officer


Key figures like Generals Elly Tumwine and Salim Saleh repotedly received-saving operations there during this period
